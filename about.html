about us


Games for my Computer is a studio and a collective
of people who make games, for hire and for fun.

We dream and experiment with alternative
methods of collaboration & game development.

Our greatest wish is to awaken the collective imagination of humanity
and usher in an era of life-affirming experimental-er games.
